Fig. 02 — The Method

Precision,
under the lens.

Prosthodontics is a discipline of magnification. We work under the lens — from loupes to the microscope — refining every margin, contact and contour at a scale the naked eye cannot resolve. Grounded in postgraduate prosthodontic training at the UCL Eastman Dental Institute and paired with today’s digital tools — intraoral scanning, CBCT, coDiagnostiX planning and CAD/CAM design — this precision lets us plan and build complex restorations to the finest tolerances.

From intraoral scanning and CBCT to coDiagnostiX-guided planning and CAD/CAM milling, the pathway is fully digital, measured and repeatable — so the smile we design is the smile we deliver.

Fig. 02 — Tooth to Implant

A tooth,
once lost.

Modern implant dentistry has transitioned from an analogue art to a precise digital science, completely revolutionising structural predictability. Utilising advanced software like coDiagnostiX, we merge Cone Beam Computed Tomography (CBCT) data with optical surface scans to execute prosthetically driven, 3D-guided implant placements. This digital ecosystem eliminates surgical guesswork, allowing for the rapid in-house printing of surgical guides that ensure optimal fixture angulation, minimal tissue trauma, and an exact foundation for your final restoration.

Plate 03 — The Craft

What we build.

Every smile is engineered as a unique biomechanical system, requiring heavily customised treatment planning. We leverage state-of-the-art additive manufacturing and optical scanning to achieve maximum clinical predictability while preserving your natural aesthetic characteristics. By maintaining complete digital control over the prototyping phase—from diagnostic wax-ups to provisional restorations—we ensure that every final ceramic unit is a precise, biologically harmonious restoration tailored specifically to you.

The Architect

Dr. Athanasios
Tsiakyroudis

Athanasios Tsiakyroudis is a London-based dentist with a special interest in prosthodontics, holding an MSc in Prosthodontics from the UCL Eastman Dental Institute and a PgDip in Implant Dentistry. With over a decade of clinical experience in the United Kingdom, his expertise encompasses full-arch rehabilitations, digital smile design, and minimally invasive aesthetic techniques, including injection moulding. Committed strictly to evidence-based care, he is highly proficient in advanced digital workflows, 3D surgical planning, and CAD/CAM technologies, maintaining active professional memberships with the BSSPD and ITI.
