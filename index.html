Every smile begins as a drawing.

Before a single tooth is touched, we design your new smile as a blueprint — proportion, symmetry, the line of your lip, the balance with your face. You see the result rendered in advance, refine it with us, and only then do we build it in ceramic.

Plate 02 — The Craft

What we build.

01 / Veneers

Ceramic Veneers

Hand-layered porcelain bonded to your own teeth. Natural translucency, designed tooth by tooth to suit your face — not a uniform set, but a considered one.

◇ Minimal preparation
02 / Crowns

Full-Arch Crowns

A complete upper or lower arch rebuilt in ceramic — form, function and colour restored as a single, harmonious whole for teeth that are worn, damaged or discoloured.

◇ Form & function
03 / Implants

Full-Arch Implant Bridges

A fixed set of teeth anchored on implants for those missing most or all of a jaw. Not a denture — a permanent bridge that stays with you, and feels like your own.

◇ Fixed & permanent
04 / Transformations

Complete Smile Transformations

A full redesign that combines veneers, crowns and implants into one cohesive result — planned as a single architecture, from the first sketch to the final polish.

◇ Fully bespoke
